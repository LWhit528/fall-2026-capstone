Artificial Intelligence Tools and Human Subjects Research: The Potential Role of AI in Human Subject Research

Introduction

This project investigated the use of AI in the creation of synthetic datasets to teach enduring concepts of data provenance, documentation, transparency, privacy, bias, validation, reproducibility, and responsible reuse.

We reviewed best practices, the pros and cons of synthetic data, created our own datasets as well as case studies for potential future workshops.
